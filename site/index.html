How it works

4 steps, all by message. You never need to film anything.

  1. Send your photos

    Product, place or people, plus your logo. The more angles you send, the closer the film gets to the real thing.

  2. See a preview

    We send a low-resolution draft so you can judge the idea, the motion and the look.

  3. Ask for changes

    One round of changes is included in every video.

  4. Get it in 1080p

    The approved draft is rendered in full quality, vertical and ready to post.

More material, better film

A few good photos are enough to start. Depending on what you ask for, sending more makes a real difference to the result.

A video with a real person needs several sharp photos of their face from different angles. A product needs every side. A place needs wide shots and details. The more the AI can see, the more faithful the film.

Questions

What clients usually ask before the first video.

Do I need to film anything?

No. We work from photos you already have: product shots, photos of your place, your logo. A short phone clip helps but is never required. Depending on the idea, more material gives a better result: see what to send.

How long does it take?

You see a preview within a few days and most videos are delivered within a week. Need it faster? Delivery in 48 hours costs 30% more.

What if I don’t like the preview?

That is what the preview is for. Tell us what to change before anything is rendered in full quality. One round of changes is included; extra rounds are listed in the extras.

Can I use the videos in paid ads?

Yes. The final files are yours to post on your channels and to run as ads.

Will it look fake?

We direct every shot by hand and pick the best of several takes. Some ideas are meant to look impossible, like a giant burger on a bridge; your product itself stays faithful to the real one.

Can you use real people or faces?

Only with written permission from the person in the photo. For clinics we never generate skin results or before-and-after images.

Which formats do I get?

Vertical 9:16 in 1080p, ready for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. Horizontal or square versions are available as extras, and packs include some for free.

How do I pay?

In US dollars or euros, in advance, by card or international bank transfer. The price of your first video counts toward a pack if you order one within 14 days.
